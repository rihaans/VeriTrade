The process

Four steps, one of them ours

You list it

Describe the device, add photos, set your price. It does not go on sale yet.

We inspect it

An evaluator assesses the device and scores it out of 100. Below 40 it is rejected outright.

A buyer purchases

Payment is held, not forwarded. The listing shows the score and the evaluator's notes.

A courier delivers

Only once delivery is confirmed does the seller get paid. That is the whole guarantee.
