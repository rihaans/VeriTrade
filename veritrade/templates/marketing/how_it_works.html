Grading

Every listing is physically assessed before it can be bought. The evaluator records a score out of 100 and written notes, and both appear on the listing exactly as written.

70 – 100
Good Minor cosmetic wear at most.
40 – 69
Fair Visible wear or a noted fault, still fully working.
0 – 39
Rejected Never reaches the marketplace.

Payment and escrow

The marketplace runs on credits. When a buyer checks out, their credits are debited immediately, but the seller is not paid yet. The money is held until a courier confirms the item was delivered.

Cancel before delivery and the buyer is refunded in full and the item goes back on sale. Every movement of credits, in either direction, is written to a ledger you can read on your credits page.
